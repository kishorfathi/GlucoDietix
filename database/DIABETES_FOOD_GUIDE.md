# Quick Reference: Sri Lankan Foods for Diabetes Management

This guide helps you choose diabetes-friendly Sri Lankan foods based on Glycemic Index (GI).

## Understanding Glycemic Index (GI)

| GI Value | Category | Effect on Blood Sugar | Recommendation |
|----------|----------|----------------------|----------------|
| ≤ 55 | **Low GI** | Slow, steady rise | ✅ Best choice for diabetes |
| 56-69 | **Medium GI** | Moderate rise | ⚠️ Eat in moderation |
| ≥ 70 | **High GI** | Rapid rise | ⚠️ Limit or avoid |

## 🍛 CURRIES & PROTEIN (ව්‍යඤ්ජන / கறிகள்)

### Excellent for Diabetes ✅
All curries are generally low GI because they contain:
- **High protein** (fish, chicken, dhal)
- **High fiber** (vegetables, lentils)
- **Healthy fats** (coconut milk in moderation)

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **Dhal** | Parippu curry, Red lentils |
| **Vegetable** | Jackfruit, Brinjal, Green beans, Cabbage |
| **Fish** | Fish curry, Dried fish |
| **Chicken** | Chicken curry (without skin) |
| **Egg** | Egg curry, Boiled eggs |

**Tip**: Curries help lower the overall GI of a meal when combined with rice!

## 🥗 SAMBOLS & MALLUMS (සම්බෝල / சாம்பல்)

### Best Choices (High Fiber, Low Carbs) ✅

| Food | Benefits | Carbs (per 100g) |
|------|----------|------------------|
| **Gotukola Sambol/Mallum** | High in vitamins, very low carbs | 5.5-6.0g |
| **Mukunuwenna Mallum** | Rich in iron, antioxidants | 5.5g |
| **Pol Mallum** | Fiber-rich | 8.0g |
| **Lunumiris** | Very low carbs, capsaicin benefits | 6.0g |

### Moderate (Watch portions) ⚠️
| Food | Note | Carbs (per 100g) |
|------|------|------------------|
| **Pol Sambol** | High fat, moderate carbs | 8.5g |
| **Seeni Sambol** | Contains sugar - limit | 25.0g |

## 🍎 FRUITS (පලතුරු / பழங்கள்)

### Low GI Fruits ✅
- **Guava (Pera)** - පේර - கொய்யா
  - High fiber (5.4g per 100g)
  - Vitamin C powerhouse (228mg!)
  
- **Papaya (Papol)** - පැපොල් - பப்பாளி
  - Low carbs (10.8g per 100g)
  - Rich in vitamins A & C

### Moderate Fruits ⚠️
- **Mango (Amba)** - Small portions only
- **Banana (Ambul)** - Choose small, unripe bananas
- **Pineapple (Annasi)** - Limit to 1 cup

### Best Choice for Hydration 💧
- **King Coconut (Thambili)** - Very low carbs (3.7g per 100g)

---

## 🥜 SNACKS (කෑම වර්ග / சிற்றுண்டிகள்)

### Better Choices ✅
| Snack | Protein | Fiber |
|-------|---------|-------|
| **Parippu Wade** | 9.0g | 4.5g |
| **Wade (Uludu)** | 8.5g | 3.5g |

### Limit (Fried, High Carbs) ⚠️
- Samosa, Cutlet, Rolls
- Kokis, Kavum
- **Better alternative**: Roasted chickpeas, nuts

---

## 🍨 DESSERTS (අතුරුපස / இனிப்புகள்)

### Best Choice ✅
- **Kiri Peni (Curd)** - Low carbs (4.7g), high protein
  - **Without sugar/honey** for best results
  - Or use in very small amounts

### Occasional Treats (Small portions only) ⚠️
- Watalappan - Rich and sweet, limit to special occasions
- Kavum, Aluwa - Very high in sugar

---

## 🥤 BEVERAGES (බීම / பானங்கள்)

### Best Choices ✅
- **Plain Tea** - No sugar
- **Plain Tea with milk** - No sugar or minimal
- **King Coconut Water** - Natural electrolytes, low carbs
- **Water with lime** - Zero carbs

### Avoid ❌
- Milk tea with sugar
- Faluda (38g carbs per serving)
- Sweetened juices

## 💡 MEAL PLANNING TIPS

### 1. The Perfect Plate for Diabetes
```
🍛 1/4 Plate: Staples (prefer red rice, pittu)
🥘 1/4 Plate: Protein (fish, chicken, dhal)
🥗 1/2 Plate: Vegetables (curries, mallums, sambols)
```

### 2. Portion Control
| Food | Good Portion | Carbs |
|------|--------------|-------|
| Red Rice | 1 cup (195g) | ~54g |
| White Rice | 1/2 cup (97.5g) | ~27g |
| String Hoppers | 2 hoppers (70g) | ~17g |
| Roti | 1 roti (100g) | ~35g |

### 3. Combine Smart
- **Always eat carbs with protein** (rice + fish curry)
- **Add high-fiber vegetables** (mallums, curries)
- **Include healthy fats** (moderate coconut, not excess)

### 4. Target Carbs per Meal
- **High glucose**: Aim for ≤45g carbs per meal
- **Normal glucose**: Aim for ≤60g carbs per meal

## ⚠️ IMPORTANT REMINDERS

1. **GI is just one factor** - Also consider portion sizes and total carbs
2. **Protein + Fiber = Better control** - Always combine carbs with these
3. **Cook smart** - Steaming and boiling better than frying
4. **Stay hydrated** - Water, plain tea, king coconut
5. **Monitor regularly** - Check blood glucose to see what works for you

## 🌟 KEY TAKEAWAYS

✅ **Choose red rice over white rice** (GI 55 vs 73)
✅ **Include protein with every meal** (fish, chicken, dhal, eggs)
✅ **Load up on vegetable curries and mallums** (high fiber, low GI)
✅ **Use sambols wisely** (avoid seeni sambol, prefer gotukola)
✅ **Drink unsweetened beverages** (plain tea, king coconut)
✅ **Control portions** (measure rice, count hoppers)
✅ **Plan ahead** (aim for ≤45-60g carbs per meal)
