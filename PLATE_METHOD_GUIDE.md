# 🍽️ Healthy Plate Method Feature

## Overview
The Plate Method feature has been added to GlucoDietix to help you manage diabetes through proper portion control. This evidence-based approach divides your plate into 4 portions for balanced nutrition.

## How It Works

### The Plate Division
Your plate is divided into **4 equal portions** (quarters):

1. **🍚 1 Portion - Rice & Grains** (1/4 of plate)
   - ~150g cooked rice
   - About 1 cup
   - Or 2 roti/bread

2. **🥗 2 Portions - Vegetables** (1/2 of plate - LARGEST)
   - ~300g vegetables
   - About 2 cups
   - Fill HALF your plate with:
     - Beetroot curry
     - Carrot salad
     - Green leafy vegetables
     - Mixed vegetable curry

3. **🍖 1 Portion - Protein/Meat Curry** (1/4 of plate)
   - ~100g protein
   - 3-4 tablespoons of curry
   - Beef curry, chicken curry, fish curry

4. **🫘 1 Portion - Dhal/Lentils** (1/4 of plate)
   - ~120g dhal curry
   - About 1/2 cup
   - Parippu, lentil curry

## Features

### Visual Plate Display
- **Circular plate visualization** showing how your meal is divided
- Color-coded sections by food category
- At-a-glance view of your plate balance

### Portion Recommendations
For each food you select, the app shows:
- ✅ **Recommended portion size** (in grams and household measures)
- 📏 **Current vs. recommended amounts**
- 🎯 **Quick "Apply" button** to adjust to recommended portion
- 💡 **Helpful tips** like "1 portion (1/4 of plate) - About 1 cup cooked rice"

### Balance Checker
- 🟢 **"Balanced" badge** when your plate has proper food groups
- ⚠️ **Missing food group alerts** if something is lacking
- Suggestions to add vegetables, protein, or grains

### Examples

#### Example 1: Balanced Sri Lankan Meal
**Selected Foods:**
- White Rice - 1 cup (150g) → 1 portion
- Beetroot Curry - 2 cups (300g) → 2 portions  
- Beef Curry - 3 tablespoons (100g) → 1 portion
- Dhal Curry - 1/2 cup (120g) → 1 portion

**Result:** ✅ Balanced plate! All 4 portions filled correctly.

#### Example 2: Needs Adjustment
**Selected Foods:**
- White Rice - 2 cups (300g) → TOO MUCH
- Chicken Curry - 5 tablespoons (150g) → TOO MUCH

**Recommendations:**
- ⚠️ Reduce rice to 150g (1 cup)
- ⚠️ Add vegetables (300g) - fill half the plate
- ⚠️ Consider adding dhal curry (120g)
- 🎯 Quick "Apply" buttons to fix portions

## How to Use

1. **Track a Meal**
   - Use "Scan Plate" or "Track Meal" to select foods
   - Add all items in your meal

2. **View Recommendations**
   - In the results screen, see the "🍽️ Healthy Plate Method" card
   - Review the circular plate visualization

3. **Check Each Food Category**
   - See recommended portions for each food
   - Compare current amounts with recommendations

4. **Adjust Portions**
   - Tap "Apply" next to any food to use recommended portion
   - Or manually adjust in the meal tracker

5. **Learn More**
   - Tap "Learn about the Plate Method" for detailed guide
   - Understand why vegetables should fill half your plate

## Health Benefits

✅ **Better Blood Sugar Control**
   - Proper carbohydrate portions prevent spikes
   - More vegetables = more fiber = slower glucose absorption

✅ **Balanced Nutrition**
   - Ensures adequate protein, vegetables, and complex carbs
   - Prevents overeating high-GI foods like white rice

✅ **Easy to Follow**
   - No complicated calorie counting
   - Visual and intuitive portion control

✅ **Culturally Appropriate**
   - Works with traditional Sri Lankan meals
   - Rice, curry, and dhal combinations

## Tips for Success

1. **Always fill vegetables FIRST** - Take the largest portion
2. **Choose brown rice over white** when possible (lower GI)
3. **Limit high-fat curries** - Use coconut milk sparingly
4. **Add variety** - Different colored vegetables provide more nutrients
5. **Stay hydrated** - Drink water, not sugary beverages
6. **Check balance indicator** - Aim for the "Balanced" badge

## Technical Details

### Food Categorization
The app automatically categorizes foods:
- **Rice & Grains**: rice, bread, roti, grains
- **Vegetables**: all vegetable curries, salads, leafy greens
- **Protein**: meat, fish, chicken, egg curries
- **Dhal**: lentils, dhal curry, parippu, beans

### Portion Calculations
- Standard plate = 4 portions
- Each portion = ~150g (1/4 of plate)
- Vegetables = double portion (300g, 1/2 plate)
- Liquid curries slightly less (120g dhal)

### Tolerance
- ±20g tolerance for "correct portion" status
- Allows flexibility while maintaining balance
